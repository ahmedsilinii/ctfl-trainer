Full mock exams

Every paper below is built to the shape of the four official ISTQB sample exams: 40 questions worth one mark each, split 8 / 6 / 4 / 11 / 9 / 2 across chapters 1 to 6, with eight K3 questions in the eight slots that always carry them, six to eight K1 questions, and one to three questions that ask for two answers. No paper draws two questions from the same diagram or table, every question stands on its own, and the options are shuffled the way the real exam shuffles them. The pass mark is 26 of 40.

The standard sitting is 60 minutes. Candidates taking the exam in a language that is not their native language are normally granted 25% extra, which is 75 minutes. If you are sitting the exam in English rather than your first language, practise against 75. The official sample exams group their questions by learning objective and warn that a live exam will not do that, so switch the order to shuffled once the material is solid.
